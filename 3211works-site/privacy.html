Privacy Policy

Clear data boundaries for focused software.

This policy explains how 3211 Works handles information in connection with this website and BoardGuard for Jira.

Effective: September 4, 2026Contact: privacy@3211works.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the 3211 Works website and to BoardGuard for Jira where 3211 Works determines how information is handled. Atlassian products and the Atlassian Marketplace are also governed by Atlassian's own terms and privacy practices.

2. BoardGuard data handling

BoardGuard is built on Atlassian Forge. The current release is designed to inspect Jira configuration through Atlassian APIs in the context of the signed-in user. BoardGuard does not use a separate application backend operated by 3211 Works and does not intentionally transmit Jira content to 3211 Works-operated servers.

The current product is designed as a read-only analysis tool. It presents findings such as board configuration signals, Saved Filter dependencies, owner and sharing information, current JQL, and potential change impact based on data available through Jira and the user's permissions.

Atlassian may process operational, platform, security, or diagnostic data as part of providing Atlassian Cloud and Forge. Those activities are governed by Atlassian's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

3. Information you send to us

If you contact support, security, or privacy by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message content, and any attachments. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate issues, maintain service quality, and meet legal or security obligations.

Please do not send passwords, API tokens, authentication secrets, or unnecessary Jira content.

4. Website data

This website is designed as a simple informational site and does not intentionally use advertising trackers or behavioral analytics. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for security, reliability, and delivery of the website.

5. Retention

Support and business correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, maintain appropriate records, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the communication.

6. Sharing

3211 Works does not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers when necessary to operate the business, provide support, host this website, or comply with law. Service providers are expected to process information only for the relevant service purpose.

7. International processing

Some infrastructure and service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the safeguards and contractual arrangements provided by the relevant service provider and applicable law.

8. Your requests

For questions or requests relating to information held directly by 3211 Works, contact privacy@3211works.com. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products, legal obligations, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
