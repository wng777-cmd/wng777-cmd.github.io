Support hours

Monday through Friday, 09:00–18:00 Korea Standard Time (KST, UTC+9), excluding public holidays in South Korea.

We aim to acknowledge ordinary support requests within two business days. Security reports that indicate a credible urgent risk are prioritized.

What to include

  • Product name and version, if known.
  • Your Atlassian product and a short description of the issue.
  • Steps that reproduce the behavior, when possible.
  • Relevant screenshots with confidential information removed or redacted.
  • For BoardGuard, whether the finding concerns board configuration, Saved Filters, permissions, or dependency impact.

Before sending Jira data

Please do not email passwords, API tokens, authentication secrets, or unnecessary Jira content. If diagnostic information is needed, we will ask for the minimum information required to investigate.

Marketplace purchases

When a 3211 Works app is purchased through Atlassian Marketplace, billing, subscription administration, and Marketplace licensing are also subject to Atlassian's Marketplace processes and terms.
